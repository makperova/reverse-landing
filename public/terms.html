6.6 Connected accounts

You can connect accounts you hold at other banks through Plaid. This allows the App and Spirit to show you your balances and transactions across your accounts. You can disconnect an account at any time in the App.

7.2 Actions Spirit takes on your behalf

Spirit can move money only on your instruction:

  • when you ask it to, for example "send 500 euros to Anna" or "move 300 euros to my tax pocket"; or

Spirit can move money between your own accounts and pockets and send payments to other people. Before sending a payment to another person, Spirit shows you the recipient and the amount in the chat and asks you to confirm. An instruction you give Spirit, or a rule you set up, is your payment instruction to Equals, and you are responsible for it. Please check the recipient details and amounts carefully. If you give a wrong recipient or amount, the payment may not be recoverable.
